Avoid
Bouncing the bar off your chest
Flaring your elbows out to 90°
Common problems
Your shoulder blades are probably drifting forward. Squeeze them back and down before you unrack, tuck your elbows to about 45°, lower to your lower chest, and drop the weight for 2 sessions. If it hurts (not just clicks), stop and see a physio.
Hands just wider than shoulder width, so your forearms are straight up and down at the bottom. Too wide stresses the shoulders. Too narrow shifts work to the triceps.
A small natural arch is good and keeps your shoulders safe. Keep your bum on the bench and feet flat. No need for a big powerlifting arch.
Lower to your lower chest and press up and slightly back so the bar finishes over your shoulders, not over your face.
